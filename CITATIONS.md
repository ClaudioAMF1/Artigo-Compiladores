This manifest is the hand-off package for sharing the literature with collaborators (e.g., Murillo). For each reference it lists the DBLP key, authors, venue, year, and the route to obtain the PDF (arXiv direct link, or DOI to resolve via the CAPES proxy on IEEE Xplore / ACM DL).

## How to download the PDFs

- **arXiv entries** (CoRR): open `https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>` and click *Download PDF*. Free, no login.
- **IEEE / ACM entries**: open `https://doi.org/<DOI>`; if it asks for payment, go through the CAPES proxy (`...periodicos.capes.gov.br`) while logged in to your institution, or search the title on Google Scholar for an open-access copy.
- **Books / PEP / blog / monograph**: links are in the table; books via library, the UFERSA monograph is open-access on the university repository.

## Verification note

arXiv (CoRR) DBLP keys are exact. Conference/journal DBLP key *suffixes* (author initials) were generated offline and should get a one-pass check on dblp.org via the search link in the last column. Four arXiv author lists (FasterPy, the survey, SysLLMatic, ECO) are placeholders — confirm against the arXiv page.
